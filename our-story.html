Our story

Brewed with
belonging.

Aravali began with a simple question: what if a cafe felt a little more like home?

2017

We opened our first door on a quiet lane in Ajmer with three tables, one borrowed espresso machine, and an unreasonable love for good coffee.

Today, our little cafe is still a place to pause. We partner with clients we know, celebrate local makers, and keep the playlist just loud enough to make strangers become friends.
