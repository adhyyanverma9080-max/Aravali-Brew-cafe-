Our story

Brewed with
belonging.

Aravali began with a simple question: what if a cafe felt a little more like home? Founded in 2017, Aravali Brew Cafe was born out of a passion for artisan brews and genuine human connection. Every cup we serve is crafted with care, inviting you to slow down, stay a while, and feel right at home.

2017

We opened our first door on a quiet lane in Ajmer with three tables, one borrowed espresso machine, and an unreasonable love for good coffee.

Today, our little cafe is still a place to pause. We partner with clients we know, celebrate local makers, and keep the playlist just loud enough to make strangers become friends.
